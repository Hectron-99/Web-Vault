How maintenance works

Your monthly fee keeps your site running, bug-free and looked after. Here is how billing works.

Monthly fee

Paid at the start of each month. I keep your site online, check it for bugs and fix them, and reply to your messages within 24 hours. A backup copy is kept, and the edits in your plan are included.

Extras

Changes beyond your included edits are billed at month end from a running list, using a price list we agree on first. Big jobs are paid half upfront.

Weekly option

If cash is tight, split the same monthly fee into weekly payments. The first week is paid upfront.

Edits and new listings

A price change, new photo or edited description counts as one edit. Replacing an old listing with a new one also counts as one edit. Need more listings than your plan includes? Each extra listing is a ₦500 setup fee.

Your site, managed for you

Your website is hosted and managed on my account, so you can focus on your business. If you stop paying the monthly fee, I stop updates to your site, and it goes offline after 2 weeks. It comes back online as soon as you pay what you owe. You can ask me at any time to hand your website over to you, and for now, that handover is free.
